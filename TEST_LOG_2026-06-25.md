## Calibration Settings Validated

- PI effort is `u = u_adelanto + direction * u_PI`.
- `direction=+1`; the sign is carried by the physical stage gain.
- HAMMER PGA gain is signed and dynamic: `1 - GainDirecta`.
- HAMMER LP gain is fixed positive `+6`, because the MFB VDAC reference enters
  through the non-inverting input.
- HAMMER PGA target: `1024 mV`.
- HAMMER LP target: `3500 mV`.
- HAMMER LP adelanto: `1280 mV` (`80` VDAC8 codes).
- HAMMER PGA and HAMMER LP lock samples: `512`.
- HAMMER LP/PGA use `FIR_calibration` in the hardware `Filter` during
  calibration and restore `FIR_adquisition` at the end.
- Button-triggered calibration is enabled.
- VDACs are initialized at their adelanto/feedforward values during PSoC boot.
- The DMA path in use is the 3-DMA design:
  `DMA_DelSig_RAM`, `DMA_DelSig_Filter`, and `DMA_Filter_RAM`.
- `AMux_ADC` signal channels are selected exclusively. A capacitor channel is
  only connected if the hardware exposes an extra channel beyond the signal
  channels; in current HAMMER there are two signal channels, so LP is not
  accidentally shorted as a capacitor. The capacitor helper is explicitly
  disconnected at calibration completion.
